# Privacy Policy — GigAlertPro

GigAlertPro monitors public posts on Reddit (via RSS) and messages in Discord channels where the bot has been explicitly invited and authorized by a server administrator. Only messages that match configured job/gig keywords are stored.

**What we collect:** message content, author display name, server name, channel name, and message link for matched messages.

**Retention:** Discord-sourced items are retained for up to 24 hours. Reddit snapshots are cached for approximately 15 minutes.

**Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, or share collected data with third parties.

**Removal:** To request removal of stored data or to have the bot removed from your server, contact the project maintainer or remove the bot from your server directly.

_Last updated: March 2026_
